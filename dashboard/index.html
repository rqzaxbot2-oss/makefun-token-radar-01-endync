      Radar Screening & Valuation Methodology

      1. Tokenless & Non-Derivative Filtering

      Every DefiLlama protocol in the sealed snapshot is audited for native token status (symbol, Gecko ID, CMC ID, market cap, token address). Protocols representing wrapped assets, liquid staking/restaking receipts, bridged versions, or sub-products of already-tokenized parent protocols are excluded so the board isolates genuine pre-TGE opportunities.

      2. Peer-Anchored Implied Launch FDV

      Base implied FDV anchors each protocol's TVL against sector-specific peer FDV/TVL multiples calibrated from tokenized comparables in the same snapshot, then adjusts for scale elasticity, 7d/30d TVL momentum, multi-chain distribution, category monetization, and observable incentive signals to produce Bear, Base, and Bull ranges plus estimated community airdrop pools.

      3. Actionable Farming Playbooks

      Each ranked protocol receives a tailored, category- and chain-aware farming sequence detailing capital positioning, activity cadence, point multipliers, and sybil-hygiene safeguards. Checklist progress saves locally in your browser with zero external network calls.
